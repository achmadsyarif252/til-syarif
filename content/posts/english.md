Kumpulan kosakata bahasa Inggris level mahir (advanced) beserta arti dan contoh penggunaannya dalam kalimat.

| Kata | Kelas | Arti | Contoh |
|------|-------|------|--------|
| **ubiquitous** | adj | ada di mana-mana | Smartphones have become **ubiquitous** in modern life. |
| **ephemeral** | adj | sekejap, tidak bertahan lama | Fame on social media is often **ephemeral**. |
| **meticulous** | adj | sangat teliti | She is **meticulous** about checking every detail of her reports. |
| **pragmatic** | adj | praktis, berorientasi pada hasil nyata | We need a **pragmatic** solution, not a perfect one. |
| **ambiguous** | adj | ambigu, bermakna ganda | The contract's wording was **ambiguous**, so both sides argued over it. |
| **resilient** | adj | tangguh, cepat pulih | Children are often more **resilient** than adults expect. |
| **exacerbate** | v | memperparah | Stress can **exacerbate** existing health problems. |
| **mitigate** | v | mengurangi dampak | Planting trees helps **mitigate** the effects of flooding. |
| **scrutinize** | v | menelaah dengan saksama | Auditors **scrutinize** every transaction. |
| **alleviate** | v | meringankan | This medicine will **alleviate** the pain. |
| **substantiate** | v | membuktikan dengan bukti | Can you **substantiate** your claim with data? |
| **procrastinate** | v | menunda-nunda | I tend to **procrastinate** when a task feels overwhelming. |
| **serendipity** | n | keberuntungan tak terduga | Meeting my mentor was pure **serendipity**. |
| **dichotomy** | n | pembagian dua hal yang bertentangan | There is a false **dichotomy** between work and happiness. |
| **paradigm** | n | kerangka berpikir, pola dasar | The internet caused a **paradigm** shift in communication. |
| **nuance** | n | nuansa, perbedaan halus | He understands the **nuances** of the language. |
| **conundrum** | n | teka-teki, dilema sulit | Choosing between two great job offers is a real **conundrum**. |
| **ostensibly** | adv | konon, tampaknya (padahal belum tentu) | He left early, **ostensibly** to see a doctor. |
| **inevitably** | adv | tak terelakkan | Change will **inevitably** bring some resistance. |
| **notwithstanding** | prep | meskipun, walau begitu | **Notwithstanding** the rain, the event went ahead. |

## Pasangan kata yang sering tertukar

- **affect** (v, memengaruhi) vs **effect** (n, dampak): *The weather **affects** my mood; the **effect** is noticeable.*
- **imply** (menyiratkan) vs **infer** (menyimpulkan): *The speaker **implied** a delay, and we **inferred** the project was behind.*
- **ensure** (memastikan) vs **insure** (mengasuransikan): *Please **ensure** the door is locked.*

## Tips menghafal

1. Pelajari kata lewat kalimat utuh, bukan terjemahan satu kata.
2. Buat satu kalimat sendiri untuk tiap kata baru.
3. Ulangi dengan jarak waktu (*spaced repetition*), misalnya 1, 3, 7, lalu 14 hari.
